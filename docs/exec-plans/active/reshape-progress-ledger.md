## Follow-up #14: `record.ts` `import/max-dependencies` suppression removed (DONE)

- **Decision/rationale:** remove the suppression rather than keep it. WI-R03 pushed `record.ts` to 16 imports, over the oxlint `import/max-dependencies` ceiling, and the engineer had suppressed it. MP's posture is that a hard ceiling is an error to fix by decomposition rather than a disable, and this production codebase carries no other such suppression.
- **Implementation:** `engineer` agent. Root-caused the over-ceiling import count: all 16 modules were all used (no orphan to drop from the WI-R03 refactor), so rather than splitting create vs transition, the fix routes the transition collision preflight `assertTransitionTargetAvailable` through the store's root-safe `store.pathExists(targetPath)` instead of a local `node:fs` `access` on a hand-built absolute path. - **Why not a split:** MP's ordering was reduce-before-split, with the create/transition split as the conditional fallback only if the count was irreducible.
